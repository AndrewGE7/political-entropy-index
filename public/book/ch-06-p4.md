
**Why Roman Institutions Lasted So Long:**
1. **Gradual Evolution:** Institutions evolved over centuries
2. **Strong Legitimacy:** Public respected and supported institutions
3. **Effective Checks:** Multiple power centers balanced each other
4. **Flexible Adaptation:** Institutions adapted to new challenges (for a while)
5. **Shared Values:** Strong civic culture and patriotism

**Why They Ultimately Failed:**
1. **Lack of Maintenance:** No major reforms after 133 BCE
2. **Economic Inequality:** Wealth concentrated, social base eroded
3. **Military Changes:** Professional army compromised institutional balance
4. **Elite Corruption:** Senate served private interests over public good
5. **External Pressures:** Expansion created strains that institutions couldn't handle
