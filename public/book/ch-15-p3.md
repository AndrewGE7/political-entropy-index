### The Arab Spring: The Entropy Wave That Reshaped the Middle East

#### The Spark That Lit a Region on Fire

On **December 17, 2010**, **Mohamed Bouazizi**—a **Tunisian street vendor**—**set himself on fire** after **police confiscated his wares** and **humiliated him**. His **act of desperation** **sparked** the **Arab Spring**, a **wave of protests** that **swept across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA)**.

```
Initial Spark (Tunisia - Dec 2010)
    ↓ (Social Media Amplification)
Protest Diffusion (Egypt, Libya, Syria - Jan-Feb 2011)
    ↓ (Authoritarian Crackdown)

    ↓ (External Intervention)

    ↓ (Power Vacuum & Chaos)

```

**Why the Arab Spring Spread So Fast:**

1. **Social Media Acceleration:**
   - **Facebook, Twitter, YouTube** enabled **rapid mobilization**
   - **Hashtags** (#Jan25, #Syria) **unified protests**
   - **Live streaming** exposed **government brutality**
   - **PEI Impact (Information Flow):** +12 points

2. **Demographic Pressure:**
   - **60% of MENA population** under **25 years old**
   - **Youth unemployment** at **30-50%**
   - **Education without opportunity** = **Revolutionary potential**
   - **PEI Impact (Economic Inequality):** +10 points

3. **Authoritarian Fragility:**
   - **Corrupt regimes** (Mubarak, Gaddafi, Assad) **lost legitimacy**
   - **Security forces** **split** between **loyalty and defection**
   - **No succession plans** = **Power vacuums**
   - **PEI Impact (Institutional Stability):** +15 points

4. **External Opportunism:**
   - **USA & Europe** initially **supported protests**
   - **Gulf States** (Qatar, Saudi Arabia) **backed different sides**
