**The Entropy Cascade Mechanism:**

1. **Local Entropy Accumulation (2000-2006):**
   - **Subprime mortgages** (high-risk loans) **accumulated** in the US housing market
   - **Financial innovation** (CDOs, CDS) **masked** the **true risk**
   - **Regulatory failure** allowed **entropy to build** unchecked
   - **PEI Impact (USA - Economic):** +3 points

2. **Entropy Amplification (2006-2007):**
   - **Housing bubble burst** in **2006**
   - **Securitized mortgages** spread **risk globally**
   - **Leverage ratios** of **30:1** or **40:1** magnified **small losses** into **catastrophic failures**
   - **PEI Impact (Global - Economic):** +8 points

3. **Entropy Transmission (2007-2008):**
   - **Bear Stearns collapse (March 2008)**—first **major warning sign**
   - **Lehman Brothers bankruptcy (September 2008)**—the **tipping point**
   - **Credit markets froze**—**liquidity crisis** spread **worldwide**
   - **PEI Impact (Global):** +12 points

4. **Entropy Export (2008-2009):**
   - **Global recession** hit **every major economy**
   - **Exports collapsed** by **20-30%** in **developed nations**
   - **Unemployment soared**—**youth unemployment** reached **50%+** in **Spain, Greece**
   - **PEI Impact (Global):** +17 points (peak)

5. **Entropy Feedback (2009-2012):**
   - **Austerity measures** in **Europe** led to **social unrest**
   - **Tea Party (USA)** and **Occupy Wall Street**—**populist backlash**
   - **Sovereign debt crises** in **Greece, Ireland, Portugal**
   - **PEI Impact (Political Dimension):** +10 points

**Why the Crisis Was a Thermodynamic Phase Transition:**

The 2008 crisis was not just a **cyclical downturn**—it was a **structural shift** in the **global economic system**:

- **From Stability to Instability:** The **post-war economic order** (Bretton Woods II) **collapsed**
- **From Order to Disorder:** The **neoliberal consensus** (free markets, deregulation) was **discredited**
