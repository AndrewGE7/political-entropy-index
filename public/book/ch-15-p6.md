### Further Reading

#### Books

**The Financial Crisis and Economic Entropy:**
- **The Big Short: Inside the Doomsday Machine** – Michael Lewis (2010)
  - *A gripping account of the 2008 financial crisis, focusing on the few who saw it coming.*
- **Too Big to Fail: The Inside Story of How Wall Street and Washington Fought to Save the Financial System—and Themselves** – Andrew Ross Sorkin (2009)
  - *A detailed narrative of the crisis and the government's response.*
- **This Time Is Different: Eight Centuries of Financial Folly** – Carmen Reinhart & Kenneth Rogoff (2009)
  - *A historical analysis of financial crises, showing that they follow predictable patterns.*
- **The Ascent of Money: A Financial History of the World** – Niall Ferguson (2008)
  - *A sweeping history of money and finance, from ancient times to the modern era.*
- **Capital in the Twenty-First Century** – Thomas Piketty (2013)
  - *A groundbreaking analysis of wealth and inequality over the past 250 years.*

**The Rise of China and Geopolitical Entropy:**
- **The Hundred-Year Marathon: China's Secret Strategy to Replace America as the Global Superpower** – Michael Pillsbury (2015)
  - *A controversial argument that China has a long-term strategy to surpass the USA.*
- **Destined for War: Can America and China Escape Thucydides's Trap?** – Graham Allison (2017)
  - *An analysis of the historical pattern of rising powers challenging established ones.*
- **China's Second Continent: How a Million Migrants Are Building a New Empire in Africa** – Howard W. French (2014)
  - *A look at China's growing influence in Africa.*
- **The China Model: Political Meritocracy and the Limits of Democracy** – Daniel A. Bell (2015)
  - *An exploration of China's alternative political model.*
- **The New Class War: Saving Democracy from the Managerial Elite** – Michael Lind (2020)
  - *An argument that the rise of a globalized elite has fueled populism and instability.*

- **The War on Peace: The End of Diplomacy and the Decline of American Influence** – Ronan Farrow (2018)
