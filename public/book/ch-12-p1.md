### World War I: The Entropy Explosion (1914-1918)

#### Thermodynamic Model: War as Systemic Phase Transition

World War I can be understood as a **thermodynamic phase transition** in the European political system. The **entropy accumulation** of the Long 19th Century (1876-1914) had pushed the system to a **critical threshold** where a **single trigger event** (the Sarajevo assassination) was sufficient to **detonate a chain reaction** of collapse and transformation.

**Phase Transition Model:**
```
Pre-War System (1914)
    ↓
Critical Threshold: PEI > 75
    ↓
Trigger Event: Sarajevo Assassination (June 28, 1914)
    ↓
Entropy Cascade: Alliance activation, mobilization
    ↓
Phase Transition: Peace → War
    ↓
New System State: Total War
```

**Why the System Was Primed for Explosion:**

By 1914, the European political system had **accumulated entropy** in all six PEI dimensions:

**The Entropy Gap:** While individual countries' PEI scores increased by only 5-8 points, the **systemic PEI** (measuring the entire European system) increased by **15-20 points** due to **interconnectedness** and **interdependence**.

#### The Home Front: Entropy Generation at Home

While the **Western Front** was the most visible site of entropy generation, the **home front** was where much of the **political entropy** was produced.

**Home Front Entropy Drivers:**

1. **Economic Entropy:**
   - **Inflation:** Consumer prices increased by **50-200%** in most countries
   - **Debt:** National debts increased by **1000-2000%**
   - **Unemployment:** Post-war demobilization created economic shock
   - **PEI Impact:** +3-5 points per country

2. **Social Entropy:**
   - **Class Conflict:** War profits vs. worker sacrifices
   - **Gender Roles:** Women entered the workforce in unprecedented numbers
   - **Generational Divide:** Young soldiers vs. older civilians
   - **PEI Impact:** +2-4 points per country

3. **Political Entropy:**
   - **Censorship:** Government control of information increased
   - **Propaganda:** Systematic manipulation of public opinion
   - **Repression:** Dissent was suppressed in the name of national unity
   - **PEI Impact:** +2-3 points per country
