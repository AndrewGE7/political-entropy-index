### The 2008 Financial Crisis: Economic Entropy Cascade

#### The Housing Bubble: Entropy Accumulation in the Financial System

The **2008 Financial Crisis** was the **greatest economic entropy cascade** since the **Great Depression**. It began with the **collapse of the US housing bubble** and **spread globally**, **destabilizing** the **global financial system** and **plunging** the **world into recession**.

**The Housing Bubble (2000-2006):**

The **US housing bubble** was **fueled by** a **combination of factors**:

1. **Low Interest Rates:** The **Federal Reserve** kept **interest rates low** (1% in 2003-2004) to **stimulate** the **economy** after the **dot-com crash** and **9/11**
2. **Deregulation:** The **1999 Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act** **repealed** the **Glass-Steagall Act**, allowing **commercial banks** to **engage in investment banking**
3. **Subprime Lending:** Banks **issued mortgages** to **borrowers with poor credit** (subprime loans), **fueling** the **housing boom**
4. **Securitization:** Banks **bundled mortgages** into **mortgage-backed securities (MBS)** and **sold them** to **investors**, **spreading** the **risk**
5. **Leverage:** Financial institutions **borrowed heavily** to **invest in MBS**, **increasing** their **exposure** to **housing market risk**

1. **Housing Prices Fell:** Home prices **began to decline** (first time since the **Great Depression**)
2. **Mortgage Defaults Rose:** Subprime borrowers **began to default** on their loans
3. **MBS Values Collapsed:** The **value of mortgage-backed securities** **plummeted**
4. **Financial Institutions Failed:** Banks and investment firms **lost billions** on **MBS investments**

**The Collapse of Lehman Brothers (September 15, 2008):**

The **collapse of Lehman Brothers**—the **fourth-largest investment bank** in the USA—was the **tipping point** of the **financial crisis**:

- **Assets:** $691 billion
- **Debt:** $613 billion
- **Employees:** 25,000
- **Impact:** **Global financial panic**

**Why Lehman's Collapse Was a Tipping Point:**

1. **Size:** Lehman was **too big to fail**—its **collapse** **threatened** the **entire financial system**
2. **Interconnectedness:** Lehman had **thousands of counterparties** around the world
3. **Leverage:** Lehman was **highly leveraged** (30:1 debt-to-equity ratio)
4. **MBS Exposure:** Lehman had **massive exposure** to **mortgage-backed securities**

**Global Financial Contagion:**

The **collapse of Lehman Brothers** **triggered a global financial panic**:

1. **Stock Markets Crashed:**
   - **Dow Jones:** -777 points on **September 29, 2008** (largest single-day point drop in history)

   - **Global Markets:** **Trillions of dollars** in **wealth wiped out**

2. **Credit Markets Froze:**
   - **Interbank Lending:** Banks **stopped lending** to each other, **fearing counterparty risk**
   - **Commercial Paper:** The **market for short-term corporate debt** **collapsed**
   - **Consumer Credit:** **Credit card limits** were **cut**, **loans became harder** to obtain

3. **Banks Failed:**
   - **USA:** **25 banks failed** in 2008, **150+** in 2009
   - **Europe:** **Dexia (Belgium/France)**, **Fortis (Netherlands/Belgium)**, **HBOS (UK)** required **bailouts**
   - **Iceland:** **Entire banking system collapsed**

4. **Recession Spread:**
   - **USA:** GDP **contracted** by **4.3%** (2008-2009)

   - **Global:** GDP **growth slowed** to **0.1%** (2009)
