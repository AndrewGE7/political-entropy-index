
7. **Wealth vs. Income:** The PEI uses income inequality, but wealth inequality is much higher. Should the PEI incorporate wealth inequality? How?

8. **Global Inequality:** The chapter focuses on within-country inequality. How does global inequality affect political entropy? Should it be included in the PEI?

9. **Technology:** How do you think emerging technologies (AI, automation, biotechnology) will affect economic inequality and political entropy?

10. **Your Country:** Calculate or estimate the Gini coefficient for your country. How has it changed over time? What are the political effects of these changes?
