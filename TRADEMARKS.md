only Age Old LLC can complete.

## What other people may do

- Cite the marks to refer to the canonical work (nominative fair use).
- Write “PEI (Political Entropy Index, Age Old LLC)” in a paper.
- Publish Adapter Manifests that *overlay* PEI, labeled as unofficial.

## What other people may not do

- Name a product, course, dataset, or repository “PEI”, “Political Entropy
  Index”, or “Age Old” in a way that suggests official status.
- Alter the logo, wordmark, or dashboard chrome and pass it off as PEI.
- Register confusingly similar domain names or social handles.

## Enforcement

Age Old LLC is the only party that may license the marks. Unofficial forks
of the source, even if technically possible on GitHub, are not PEI.
