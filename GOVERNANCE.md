# Governance — Age Old Research

**Steward:** Age Old LLC, Andrew Eppler, sole editor of the Canonical Work.

**Division:** Age Old Research exists to keep PEI as a public research
instrument without surrendering editorial control of the book or the index.

## Decision rights

| Subject | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Manuscript, formula, weights, phase bands | Age Old LLC only |
| Dashboard and curriculum source | Age Old LLC only |
| Trademark licensing | Age Old LLC only |
| Certified adapters | Age Old LLC, discretionary |
| Unofficial Adapter Manifests | Their authors, under §4 of LICENSE |
| Citations and papers using PEI | Researchers, with attribution |

## Why this is not OSI “open source”

OSI open source requires the right to modify and redistribute modified
works. That right would let a fork change the PEI weights or the book and
still call it PEI. The Age Old Research License withholds that right on
purpose. Researchers still get:

- the full source to inspect;
- the right to run it;
- the right to connect their own data through the adapter schema;
- the right to cite.

That is **open research, source-available, no unofficial derivatives**.

## Releases

Canonical releases are tagged `pei-vMAJOR.MINOR` from `main` by the owner.
Anything else is not PEI.
